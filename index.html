Is CareerMaxx free?

Yes, you can start for free. Paid plans at $4.99 and $9.99 per month add résumé analysis and AI tools.

Who is it for?

Engineering students, especially first-year and early-career students looking for co-ops, internships and extracurricular experience.

Which opportunities do you list?

Co-ops, internships, student clubs and free programs in the Ottawa area.

Is my résumé kept private?

Yes, always. Your résumé is stored only in your own account and is never shared. You can replace it or delete it whenever you want.
